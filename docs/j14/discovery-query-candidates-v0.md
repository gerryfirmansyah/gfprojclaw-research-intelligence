Status: PROPOSED ONLY. Nothing in this document is canonical project configuration and nothing here authorizes live retrieval.

## Design rule
Queries are deliberately broader than a claimed gap and are intended to discover possible supporting, adjacent, and counter-evidence. They are metadata-discovery expressions, not scientific conclusions. Provider syntax will be encoded by adapters without changing the HUMAN-approved semantic term groups.

## Profile A — Computer / Information Systems
Canonical direction under investigation concerns empirical/explanatory specificity of AI in public governance. The existing Profile A terminology already includes digital government, e-government, digital public administration/governance, public-sector information systems, and technology governance.

Proposed semantic groups:
- AI group: `artificial intelligence`, `AI`, `algorithmic systems`, `automated decision making`
- public-governance context: `digital government`, `e-government`, `public administration`, `public sector`, `government`
- governance/explanation group: `governance`, `accountability`, `decision making`, `implementation`, `institutional`, `organizational`

Candidate discovery expression:
`("artificial intelligence" OR "algorithmic systems" OR "automated decision making") AND ("digital government" OR "e-government" OR "public administration" OR "public sector" OR government) AND (governance OR accountability OR "decision making" OR implementation OR institutional OR organizational)`

Guardrail: this query does not assert that empirical/explanatory research is absent or underdeveloped. Results may challenge the current candidate direction.

## Profile B — Management / Organization Studies
Canonical investigation direction concerns cross-level mechanisms linking employee attributes/human capability to organizational resilience. Existing Profile B terminology includes organizational/organisational resilience, resilient organization, human capability, employee/workforce capability, employee resilience, adaptive capacity, organizational adaptation, dynamic capabilities, and human capital.

Proposed semantic groups:
- resilience group: `organizational resilience`, `organisational resilience`, `resilient organization`, `resilient organisation`
- human group: `human capability`, `human capabilities`, `employee capability`, `workforce capability`, `human capital`, `employee resilience`
- mechanism/level group: `mechanism`, `cross-level`, `multilevel`, `adaptive capacity`, `adaptation`, `behavior`, `behaviour`

Candidate discovery expression:
`("organizational resilience" OR "organisational resilience" OR "resilient organization" OR "resilient organisation") AND ("human capability" OR "human capabilities" OR "employee capability" OR "workforce capability" OR "human capital" OR "employee resilience") AND (mechanism OR "cross-level" OR multilevel OR "adaptive capacity" OR adaptation OR behavior OR behaviour)`

Guardrail: this query investigates relationships and adjacent terminology; it does not assert a causal mechanism, research gap, novelty, or significance.
