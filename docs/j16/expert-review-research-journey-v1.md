## Core conclusion
Fondasi journey sudah sehat: HUMAN authority, provenance, machine observation, serta invariant bahwa Investigation Opportunity tidak otomatis menjadi validated gap.

Review expert menambahkan lima penguatan utama: Evidence Qualification sebelum analysis; Evidence Synthesis sebelum IO; deliberate falsification/counter-search; Research Question formation sebagai tahap eksplisit; Knowledge Evolution dipindahkan sesudah keputusan ilmiah HUMAN.

## Product boundary
**Research Workspace** menjawab: *Apa yang layak saya investigasi dan mengapa?*
Ia memiliki discovery, evidence construction, screening, qualification, analysis, synthesis, candidate IO, focused investigation, HUMAN evidence assessment, dan HUMAN direction.

**Research Copilot v2** menjawab: *Bagaimana investigasi ini menjadi research problem, defensible RQ, theoretical positioning, method, design, contribution, dan research-ready project?*
Ia selalu dimulai dari Research Reasoning Package dan tidak boleh mencampur stale project/session state.

## Corrected sequence
Workspace W1–W13:
Research Universe & Landscape → Area/Sub-area Exploration → Screening Strategy → HUMAN Evidence Selection → Evidence Qualification → Model Analysis → Compare/Contrast/Cross-dimensional Synthesis → Candidate Investigation Opportunities → HUMAN IO Selection → Focused Investigation + Counter-search → HUMAN Evidence Assessment → Focused Synthesis → HUMAN Direction.

Copilot C1–C16:
Session Brief/Problem Context → Investigation Reasoning → Evidence Verification & Provenance → Boundary/Coverage/Falsification → HUMAN Decision Checkpoint → Research Problem + Candidate RQ → HUMAN RQ Decision → Concept/Construct Clarification → Theory Positioning → Method Intelligence → Research Design → Contribution Formation → Novelty Challenge → Argument/Evidence Audit → Research Readiness → Knowledge Evolution/Radar.

## Non-linear loops
Evidence insufficient → W4/W5.
Counter-evidence changes pattern → W2/W3/W4.
IO fails → W8.
RQ not defensible → C2/C4/C6.
Theory weak → C2/C8.
Novelty challenged → C4/C6/C9.
Design invalid/unfeasible → C10/C6.
New evidence from Radar → C3/C4/C5.
