# Simple End-to-End Research Trial v0

Status: READY FOR HUMAN TOPIC SELECTION — no production scientific mutation performed by this preparation.

## Purpose
Test whether GFPROJCLAW is genuinely useful to a researcher, not merely technically operational. The trial uses one small, real research question chosen by HUMAN and follows it from canonical context through discovery, evidence inspection, critique, HUMAN review, and the next research action.

## Scientific boundary
The trial must not pre-seed a gap, novelty, significance, causal mechanism, relevance verdict, or acceptance decision. Machine output is candidate context/advice. HUMAN remains scientific authority. Missing context stays UNKNOWN / NOT_AVAILABLE / NOT_RUN. ABSTRACT_ONLY stays explicit. No synthetic committed production scientific history is allowed. Writer `--apply` and Continuous Pilot activation remain outside this trial unless separately authorized.

## Trial size
Use one real topic with a deliberately small scope: one Profile, one Project, one provisional RQ, a small discovery slice, and only enough evidence relationships to exercise inspection and review. Prefer a question the HUMAN actually wants to understand.

## Ten-step HUMAN journey
1. **Define intent** — HUMAN chooses Profile, Project name, research intent, and optionally a provisional RQ in authenticated Admin Copilot. This creates canonical configuration versions only.
2. **Confirm orientation** — open Research Copilot and verify the selected Profile/Project and R0–R16 guidance describe the intended workspace without inventing maturity status.
3. **Discover** — run only an explicitly authorized bounded discovery slice. Record provider/source state and local failures; do not equate no result with scientific absence.
4. **What changed?** — inspect exact new paper/Claim/Advice members in Daily Attention rather than relying on aggregate counts alone.
5. **What needs attention?** — inspect NEEDS_REVIEW/CONTESTED and counter-search state; distinguish outstanding attention from newly changed items.
6. **Inspect evidence** — trace Claim → EvidenceFragment → Work/source. Verify access level, quotation/context, and provenance. ABSTRACT_ONLY is a limitation, not full-text evidence.
7. **Read quality context** — use HUMAN-readable quality cards to ask what the state means, why it matters, and what HUMAN should inspect. Counts are not quality scores.
8. **Read Advice & Critic** — inspect weaknesses, alternatives, uncertainty, coverage limitations, and suggested falsification/checking work. Advice is not a verdict.
9. **HUMAN review** — HUMAN agrees, disagrees, contests, or asks for more investigation through existing authorized controls. Record reasons where the canonical workflow supports them.
10. **Continue research** — compare the post-review attention state and identify the next bounded research action. The system should help answer what changed, what needs attention, why, where the evidence is, and what remains unknown.

## Usefulness questions
At every step record YES / PARTIAL / NO plus a short reason for:
- Can HUMAN tell what changed?
- Can HUMAN tell what needs attention?
- Can HUMAN understand why without reading raw JSON/database terminology?
- Can HUMAN reach the exact evidence/source?
- Are uncertainty, access limits, and NOT_RUN/NOT_AVAILABLE states understandable?
- Does Advice & Critic suggest useful checking work without deciding the science?
- Can HUMAN make/revise a decision with an inspectable reason?
- Is the next research action clearer after using GFPROJCLAW?

## Friction log
For each problem record: step, screenshot/observable behavior, expected researcher need, actual behavior, severity (`BLOCKER`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`), and proposed change. UX friction may be fixed during J16 if it does not change accepted scientific semantics. Scientific-model changes require an explicit architecture decision.

## Acceptance
This trial passes usefulness only by explicit HUMAN judgment after completing the journey. Machine regressions can prove traceability and boundaries but cannot declare the workflow useful. A trial PASS does not replace the deferred replacement-environment replication gate or final J16 HUMAN acceptance.
