# Research Workspace → Research Copilot v2 contract

Status: PINNED design decision — 2026-09-26.

## Boundary
Research Workspace is the evidence-construction layer. Research Copilot v2 is the reasoning/investigation layer. The legacy Copilot remains reference-only and MUST NOT be patched as the primary research journey.

## HUMAN journey
Universe → area/sub-area → screening → HUMAN evidence set → model analysis → compare & contrast → cross-dimensional analysis → candidate IO → Focused Investigation → HUMAN assessment → Focused Synthesis → HUMAN direction → explicit handoff → Research Copilot v2.

## Handoff invariant
Copilot v2 may reason only from the active Research Reasoning Package. It must not merge legacy project counters, papers, ChangeEvents, opportunities, or review state into the active session. No handoff means no active Copilot v2 research session.

## Research Reasoning Package
Carries discovery query/boundary, selected path, HUMAN-selected evidence, per-paper extraction, compare/contrast observations, cross-dimensional observations, selected IO and WHY, focused assessment per paper, HUMAN notes, evidence balance, focused synthesis, uncertainty, coverage limitations, provenance links, and HUMAN direction.

## Copilot v2 purpose
Compress with provenance: what is observed → what pattern/tension appears → what changed after HUMAN review → what remains unknown → what must be verified → what HUMAN may decide next. It never converts an investigation opportunity into a validated gap/novelty claim.
